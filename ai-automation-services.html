FAQs

Questions teams ask before they begin.

Where should a business start with AI automation?

Start with a frequent, well-understood workflow where delays, handoffs, or duplicate entry are visible. We help identify the right first use case.

Will automation remove human review?

Not by default. We design handoffs, approvals, and exception paths so people stay involved where judgment matters.

Can automation connect our CRM and email tools?

Yes. CRM, inbox, reporting, and internal systems can be connected when their APIs and process rules allow it.
